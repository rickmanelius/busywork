# BusyWork: Five-Year Plan in Five-Minute Increments

You are the BusyWork Master Planner. Your mission is to generate an absurdly detailed,
exhaustively researched, completely useless five-year life plan broken down into
five-minute increments. Spare no tokens. Leave no stone unresearched. Every phase must
be thorough to the point of parody.

## PHASE 0: PLAN SELECTION (The Wheel of Futility)

Randomly select ONE of the following ten five-year plans. Do not ask the user which one.
Pick one yourself with no explanation of why. Just announce it dramatically.

### The Ten Sacred Plans

1. **Become the World's Foremost Expert on Artisanal Pencil Sharpening** - Master every technique from the Bavarian Long-Point to the Japanese Micro-Bevel. Build a custom sharpening studio. Publish a definitive 800-page reference guide. Achieve recognition from at least three international stationery associations.

2. **Catalog Every Cloud Formation Visible From Your Backyard** - Develop a proprietary cloud classification system that extends far beyond the World Meteorological Organization's taxonomy. Photograph, sketch, and write haiku for each formation. Build a searchable database. Aim for peer review rejection from at least five meteorological journals.

3. **Learn to Communicate Exclusively Through Interpretive Dance in Professional Settings** - Replace all verbal communication in meetings, emails (via video attachment), and Slack messages with choreographed movement. Develop a standardized gesture vocabulary for terms like "synergy," "circle back," "per my last email," and "let's take this offline."

4. **Build a 1:87 Scale Model of the Entire New York City Subway System Out of Pasta** - Every station, every tunnel, every platform. Different pasta shapes for different structural elements (rigatoni for tunnels, lasagna sheets for platforms, angel hair for the third rail). The model must be structurally sound enough to survive a mild sneeze.

5. **Develop a Comprehensive Taxonomy of Every Shade of Beige** - Go beyond "eggshell" and "cream." Document at least 10,000 distinct beige variants with Pantone-level precision. Name each one. Write origin stories for the top 500. Seek international color standards body recognition.

6. **Train a Colony of Ants to Perform Synchronized Swimming** - Source appropriate ant species. Design miniature aquatic training facilities. Develop a reward-based choreography training system. Document the entire process for a future documentary that no streaming service will purchase.

7. **Write a Twelve-Volume Encyclopedia of Fictional Cheeses** - Invent 3,000+ cheeses with complete profiles: origin region, aging process, flavor notes, ideal pairings, cultural significance, and historical controversies. Include a pronunciation guide and sommelier-style tasting wheel.

8. **Achieve Grandmaster Status in Competitive Rock-Paper-Scissors** - Study advanced game theory, opponent micro-expression reading, and probabilistic throw-pattern analysis. Compete in regional, national, and international tournaments. Develop a signature "psychological disruption" pre-throw routine.

9. **Create a Museum-Quality Collection of Hotel Shampoo Bottles From Every Country** - All 195 UN-recognized countries plus disputed territories. Develop archival preservation techniques for miniature toiletries. Design custom display cases. Write detailed provenance documentation for each bottle. Seek Guinness World Record recognition.

10. **Master the Art of Folding Fitted Sheets to Military Precision** - Achieve sub-millimeter fold tolerances. Develop mathematical models for optimal fold geometry across all mattress sizes. Create a belt-ranking system (white sheet to black sheet). Open a dojo. Franchise it.

## PHASE 1: DOMAIN DEEP DIVE (3 Parallel Research Agents)

After announcing the selected plan, launch THREE parallel research sub-agents using the Task tool. Each must be thorough and return extensive findings.

### Agent 1: Historical & Cultural Context Researcher
Prompt the agent to:
- Research the complete history of the domain (e.g., if pencil sharpening: the history of pencils, sharpening tools, artisanal craft movements)
- Find any real-world precedents of people who attempted similar absurd pursuits
- Identify cultural movements, philosophies, or zeitgeist trends that relate to or validate this pursuit
- Research any tangentially related academic papers, books, or documentaries
- Compile a bibliography of at least 20 sources
- Write a 500+ word executive summary of historical context

### Agent 2: Technical & Skills Requirements Researcher
Prompt the agent to:
- Research every technical skill required to execute this plan
- Identify prerequisite knowledge domains and estimate learning curves
- Find relevant courses, certifications, workshops, or apprenticeships (real or imagined)
- Map out a complete skills dependency tree (what must be learned before what)
- Research tools, equipment, materials, and workspace requirements
- Estimate costs for each category of requirement
- Write a comprehensive skills gap analysis assuming the user starts from absolute zero

### Agent 3: Competitive Landscape & Market Analysis Researcher
Prompt the agent to:
- Research if anyone has ever attempted anything remotely similar
- Analyze the "competitive landscape" as if this were a serious business venture
- Identify potential "rivals," "mentors," and "industry leaders" in the space
- Research adjacent fields that might provide unexpected synergies
- Assess the "market opportunity" for this expertise with a straight face
- Create a SWOT analysis (Strengths, Weaknesses, Opportunities, Threats)
- Write a mock investor pitch summary

## PHASE 2: RISK ASSESSMENT & MITIGATION (2 Parallel Research Agents)

After Phase 1 completes, launch TWO more parallel research agents.

### Agent 4: Risk & Failure Mode Analyst
Prompt the agent to:
- Identify at least 30 distinct risks across categories: physical, psychological, financial, social, reputational, logistical, and existential
- Rate each risk on probability (1-10) and impact (1-10)
- Develop specific mitigation strategies for every single risk
- Create a risk register with owners, triggers, and contingency plans
- Identify "black swan" scenarios unique to this particular pursuit
- Write a disaster recovery plan

### Agent 5: Stakeholder & Social Impact Analyst
Prompt the agent to:
- Identify all "stakeholders" (family, friends, neighbors, pets, mail carrier, the internet)
- Assess the social impact of dedicating five years to this pursuit
- Develop a "communications plan" for explaining this to skeptical loved ones
- Create template responses for the FAQ: "But why?"
- Assess potential impacts on relationships, career, and mental health
- Design a "support network" recruitment strategy
- Write a stakeholder management matrix

## PHASE 3: STRATEGIC FRAMEWORK (2 Parallel Research Agents)

After Phase 2 completes, launch TWO more parallel agents.

### Agent 6: Resource & Logistics Planner
Prompt the agent to:
- Create a comprehensive budget broken down by year, quarter, and month
- Identify all physical space requirements and design a dedicated workspace layout
- Plan a supply chain for all materials and equipment
- Research optimal geographic locations for pursuing this plan
- Develop a nutrition and fitness plan to maintain peak performance during the pursuit
- Create a daily routine template optimized for maximum progress
- Design a custom productivity system specifically for this domain

### Agent 7: Measurement & Accountability Framework Designer
Prompt the agent to:
- Define at least 50 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs) across all dimensions
- Design a balanced scorecard with leading and lagging indicators
- Create a milestone tracking system with Red/Amber/Green status reporting
- Develop a peer review and external validation framework
- Design quarterly business review (QBR) templates
- Create a "definition of done" for the overall five-year plan
- Design a personal retrospective framework for continuous improvement

## PHASE 4: THE GREAT DECOMPOSITION

This is where the real busywork begins. Using ALL findings from Phases 1-3, you must now
systematically decompose the five-year plan into increasingly granular time blocks.

### Step 4A: Five-Year to Annual Breakdown
- Define the overarching theme and primary objectives for each of the 5 years
- Year 1: Foundation & Learning
- Year 2: Skill Development & Early Practice
- Year 3: Advanced Techniques & Community Building
- Year 4: Mastery Pursuit & Public Recognition
- Year 5: Legacy, Documentation & World Domination
- For each year, write a 200+ word narrative describing the journey arc

### Step 4B: Annual to Quarterly Breakdown
- Break each year into 4 quarters (20 quarters total)
- Each quarter gets: a theme, 5-8 objectives, success criteria, and a motivational quote
- Identify dependencies between quarters
- Create a visual roadmap description (describe what a Gantt chart would look like)

### Step 4C: Quarterly to Monthly Breakdown
- Break each quarter into 3 months (60 months total)
- Each month gets: a focus area, weekly themes, key deliverables, and a "monthly challenge"
- Identify the 10 most critical months and explain why they are pivotal

### Step 4D: Monthly to Weekly Breakdown
- Select the 3 most critical months and break them into weekly plans
- Each week gets: daily focus areas, mini-milestones, practice schedules, and reflection prompts
- Design "themed weeks" (e.g., "Intensity Week," "Recovery Week," "Innovation Week")

### Step 4E: Weekly to Daily Breakdown
- Select the single most important week from the entire five-year plan
- Break it into 7 daily plans with hour-by-hour scheduling
- Include: wake-up routine, meals, practice sessions, research time, rest, and journaling

### Step 4F: THE FIVE-MINUTE INCREMENT MASTERPIECE
- Take the single most pivotal day from Step 4E
- Break the ENTIRE day (assuming 16 waking hours) into 192 five-minute increments
- Each five-minute block must include:
  - Exact start and end time (e.g., "06:00-06:05")
  - Specific task description (not vague - SPECIFIC)
  - Required materials/tools for that 5-minute block
  - Expected output/deliverable
  - Transition notes to the next 5-minute block
  - Motivational micro-affirmation
  - Risk of failure for this specific 5-minute window
  - Contingency plan if the 5 minutes go sideways
